Lesson 6 • Internet & Email

Internet & Email Learning

The internet connects computers around the world. A browser is the program you use to visit websites, and email is how you send written messages to people.

What is the internet?

The internet is a network of computers connected together. With it you can read news, watch videos, study, shop, and talk to people far away. You connect through Wi-Fi or mobile data, then open a browser.

The browser — your window to the web

Popular browsers: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari. They all work the same way:

  • Address bar: the long box at the top — type a website address here, e.g. www.google.com, and press Enter.
  • Search: type words into Google to find websites about a topic, e.g. “how to make a CV”.
  • Tabs: open a new tab with Ctrl + T to keep several websites open at once.
  • Back / Forward: the arrows at the top-left go to the page you were on before.
  • Bookmark: press Ctrl + D to save a website you want to visit again.
How to search well: use clear words. Instead of “computer”, search “basic computer course for beginners”. The more exact your words, the better the results.

Safe browsing — 5 rules

  1. Check the address: real websites start with https:// and show a lock icon. Be careful with strange addresses.
  2. Do not click pop-ups that say you have won a prize or your computer is infected — they are almost always fake.
  3. Do not download files from websites you do not trust.
  4. Do not share your password, bank details or OTP (one-time code) with anyone — real companies never ask for them by message.
  5. Log out on shared or public computers.

Email — how it works

An email address looks like this: name@example.com. The part before @ is the person, the part after is the email service (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo).

Part of an emailWhat it means
ToWho you are sending it to
SubjectA short title — e.g. “Application for the computer course”
BodyThe message itself
Attach (📎)Add a file, like a photo or a CV
SendSends the email

Send your first email — step by step

  1. Open your email (Gmail, Outlook, or another service) and sign in.
  2. Click Compose / New mail.
  3. In To, type the receiver's email address carefully — one wrong letter and it goes to the wrong person.
  4. In Subject, write a short title.
  5. In the body, write politely: a greeting, your message, and your name at the end. Example: “Dear Sir, … Thank you, Reduan”.
  6. Click Send.
  7. To answer an email you received, open it and click Reply — the address and subject are filled in for you.

Strong passwords

A strong password protects your email and accounts. Good passwords are:

  • Long — at least 8 characters, longer is better.
  • Mixed — capital letters, small letters, numbers and symbols, e.g. Sun#2026!River.
  • Private — never share it, and do not use the same password for every site.
  • Not obvious — not your name, birthday or 123456.
Phishing warning: If an email says “your account will close — click here and log in now”, stop. Go to the real website yourself instead of clicking the link, and never type your password on a page that arrived by email.
